Procurement action — pricing leverage near-closed · focus on supply security
Pricing leverage is gone — this is now a supply security conversation. Nanya's trailing twelve-month revenue of ~$5.8B USD is nearly double their FY2021 peak. At 79.5% gross margin, NT$198B net cash, and management guiding further improvement, there is no financial pressure that creates a pricing concession. Do not enter this negotiation expecting to move ASPs down.

What to do instead: (1) Request explicit price floor provisions in any LTA — Nanya prices quarterly "according to market trend" by default, meaning no automatic protection from continued ASP increases without an explicit contractual floor. (2) For 2028+ programmes, engage on new fab allocation now — four major customers participated in the NT$78.7B private placement and secured supply priority. Buyers outside that circle need to initiate allocation conversations proactively before capacity is committed. (3) Validate the DDR4/LPDDR4 node roadmap for your programme duration — Nanya is building 1C/1D/1E with EUV from 2028, confirm the node your programme needs is covered. (4) Address geographic concentration in your programme risk review — Taiwan single-source for a 10–15 year automotive programme is a board-level risk, not a procurement conversation.
