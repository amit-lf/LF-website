SUPPLIER INTELLIGENCE

Your supplier knows their position. Now you do too.

A supplier’s commercial team spends the year on pricing and contract strategy for a handful of product lines. Your team covers twenty suppliers and several hundred part numbers. We close that gap using what the supplier has already published.

What you get

Four readings on every supplier we cover

Financial position

Revenue, margin, inventory days and capital spending across six filed years with the trailing quarters, from the supplier’s own accounts. Facts, not inference.

What management said

Capacity and inventory, read from the buyer’s seat rather than a shareholder’s — a supplier running flat out is a good quarter for its investors and a bad one for you. Every reading carries the sentence it came from and the quarter it was said in.

Where the room sits

How much of a supplier’s cost stays in place when your volume moves, fitted on their own filed accounts and tested against a year held out of the fit.

Ownership and exposure

Who ultimately owns the supplier, and what that means for export control and continuity. A clean screening result is reported as what it is — not covered is never shown as cleared.

How it is built

Nothing here is a forecast

We read the position a supplier is already in, from what they have already published. Quotes are matched word for word against the transcript before they are shown, and one we cannot match is dropped rather than paraphrased. Where the filings will not support an answer, the platform says so instead of estimating — a model that declines where the data runs out is worth more in a negotiation than one that guesses.

Getting it

Priced per supplier, per year

Cover the suppliers your chip spend actually concentrates in. One supplier a week is free to read with no account, so you can see the depth before you decide.
