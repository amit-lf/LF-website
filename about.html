How we work

What we will and will not claim.

Procurement teams have been sold dashboards before. So it is worth being exact about what this is.

Everything traces to a document

Every figure comes from a filing, a transcript or a public register, and carries the date it was checked. If a claim cannot be traced, it does not get made.

We do not forecast

Nothing here predicts a shortage, a price move or an event. It reads the position a supplier is already in, from what they have already published.

We say when we do not know

Where a company does not publish a figure, or an ownership record cannot be followed, the answer is “this cannot be seen here” — not a guess dressed as a finding.
