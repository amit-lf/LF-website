How we work

What we will and will not claim.

Procurement teams have been sold dashboards before. So it is worth being exact about what this is.

Everything traces to a document

Figures come from a supplier’s own filed accounts. Quotes come from earnings calls and are matched word for word against the transcript before they are shown; one we cannot match is dropped rather than paraphrased. If a claim cannot be traced, it does not get made.

We do not forecast

Nothing here predicts a shortage, a price move or an event. It reads the position a supplier is already in, from what they have already published.

Coverage grows with demand

We cover the semiconductor suppliers a buyer’s chip spend actually concentrates in, not every listed company. New names are added as buyers ask for them — so if one of yours is missing, that is a gap in our coverage and not a finding about the supplier.

We say when we do not know

Where a company does not publish a figure, or an ownership record cannot be followed, the answer is “this cannot be seen here” — not a guess dressed as a finding.
