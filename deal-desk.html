Silicon carbide

Substrate and epi, where most of a module’s cost sits. The read runs through the equipment and substrate makers, because that is where overcapacity shows up before a device maker acknowledges it.

What the upstream is saying

Aixtron’s CEO put SiC fab utilization at roughly 30% a year ago and 50–70% now — still short of the 80–85% where equipment makers expect reorders, which Aixtron does not see before 2027–28. Wolfspeed is running a negative gross margin, and its CFO has told analysts they need about $800M of revenue to break even against roughly $600M today. Capital spending fell from $104M a quarter to $5M.

What it means across the table

When an IDM says SiC costs are rising, that is their absorption problem, not your cost increase. High fixed costs spread over low volumes is their arithmetic to solve.

DRAM and enterprise memory

Memory is priced on a cycle, and the published forecasts have lagged that cycle badly. Our co-founder spent years negotiating memory pricing at Micron; the desk is built around the questions that work when the analyst number does not.

The forecasts missed, in one direction

TrendForce’s own quarterly DRAM contract-price forecasts, set against what TrendForce later reported, were out by 20 to 40 points four quarters running — every one of them under-forecasting the rise. Buyers are now signing multi-year agreements priced off the next one.

Allocation, not only price

All three makers want multi-year commitments. That is leverage for written allocation, a shorter term and a fairer price floor — if you know what each of them has already committed, and to whom.
