Silicon carbide

Open

Substrate and epi, where most of a module’s cost sits. The read runs through the equipment and substrate makers, because that is where overcapacity shows up before a device maker acknowledges it.

Aixtron put SiC fab utilization at 50–70%, short of the 80–85% where equipment makers expect reorders. So when an IDM says silicon carbide costs are rising, that is their absorption problem rather than your cost increase.
