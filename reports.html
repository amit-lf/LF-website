LinkedIn · Semitrical · 10 November 2026

Should you sign a multi‑year NAND contract?

Phison says about 70% of its 2027 NAND supply is already locked, and Kioxia says 2027 is sold out with 2028 more than half committed. Yet Kioxia’s own pricing disclosures show increases slowing sharply — from more than doubling in early 2026 to a guided 3–4% blended rise next quarter, against TrendForce’s 15–20% for the same period. Micron’s new agreements carry floor prices set at “a gross margin well above any prior peak”, and a floor protects the seller on the way down. Separate supply from price: commit volume for 2027, set price year by year.

LinkedIn · Semitrical · 5 November 2026

Is the memory upcycle cannibalizing itself?

Kioxia posted ¥2.48tn revenue at 80% operating margins, then guided next quarter flat with only “very moderate” ASP increases. Data centres are now half of flash bit shipments against 30% a year ago, so enterprise SSD pricing holds while consumer units fall away on BOM cost. Forecasts put blended NAND contract prices up 15–20% for Q4-26; Kioxia expects 3–4%. Which number applies depends on which segment you buy in.

How accurate were DRAM contract-price forecasts during this super-cycle?

TrendForce’s own quarterly forecasts against what TrendForce later reported: out by 20 to 40 points four quarters running, every one in the same direction. Written from years of negotiating memory pricing at Micron.
