2027 is already sold. Here is what is fixed, what is open, and when you have to decide.

A planning note for CPOs,
VPs of Sourcing and Supply Chain
Evidence to 3 November 2026

By their own public statements, the memory makers have already committed most of their 2027 output, and the capacity meant to relieve it does not produce inside 2027. Micron says more than 75% of fiscal 2027 shipments are committed. SK Hynix's long‑term agreements cover more than half of 2027–28 capacity. Seagate has priced the entirety of calendar 2027.

No forecast appears on this page. Every line is a dated statement a named company made about its own plans. We publish no view on where prices go; what we can tell you is what is already contracted, which turns out to be the more useful half.

3 · Four openings the suppliers have given you themselves

1 · They have said the increases moderate

Micron's CFO: fiscal 2027 margins expand on “continued price increases, albeit at a more moderate pace”, adding “we have said for some time that eventually price increases would moderate”.Micron FQ4‑26 call, 30 Sep 2026

Expect an increase, and argue about its size against the supplier's own word.

2 · They have said their prices are killing demand

SK Hynix: PC and mobile shipments are expected to fall on higher memory prices. Samsung: smartphone volumes already fell, and 2027 unit volumes are expected to decline. TSMC: price‑sensitive segments “are being challenged” by rising component prices.Q3‑26 calls, Oct 2026

The tight‑supply account is not uniform by segment, and they said it first.

3 · DDR4 has second sources adding capacity

Winbond's DRAM bits rose about 25% in one quarter as 16nm ramps, and it describes the DDR4/LPDDR4 gap as evident through 2028. Nanya's bits stay flat only until new fab output in 2H 2027.Winbond CQ1–CQ2‑26 presentations; Nanya Q3‑26 call, 12 Oct 2026

Where the part is DDR4‑class, the single‑source assumption is wrong.

4 · Kioxia is the uncommitted door

Only 50% of shipments targeted under agreement by 2028, against Samsung already at 60–70% of capacity and Micron above 75% of FY27. Kioxia's gross margin is at a six‑year high with capital spending at 12% of revenue, against its own 40% in fiscal 2022.Kioxia Q1‑26 call; filed accounts

Qualification, not availability, is the constraint here — so start it now.

4 · Three decisions, and the date each one is really due

Before FY27
commitments Prepay, or not. Samsung requires advance payments, SK Hynix deposits — a capital decision, not a procurement one. Price it against your own return on cash: it pays only if the discount beats what that cash earns while it is held. Set the annual ceiling and the approver before a negotiation forces it. Before signing
a quantity Commit capacity, not quantity. Suppliers say they are buying planning visibility. A reserved share of output with a quarterly draw inside an agreed band gives them that without you signing a number you cannot forecast. Untested: nothing public says any of them has written it that way. Q1 2027,
at the latest Open the second source while it is still open. Kioxia for NAND, Winbond and Nanya for DDR4‑class DRAM. Qualification takes longer than the window: Kioxia's agreement year is 2028, Nanya's new output 2H 2027.

5 · Two things not to plan on

The forecast you are probably using. TrendForce came in 20–40 points low in every quarter we can measure — it raised 4Q26 DDR5 from +10–15% to about +20% on 20 October, after 3Q26 settled near +40%. Its August chart has 1Q27 at about +9%, levelling from mid‑2027. The same lag is likely at the turn, which is when buyers will be locked into agreements priced off today's numbers.

The supplier's own outlook. Samsung expects it “unlikely to see any significant increase in incremental supply through 2028”; SK Hynix calls the constraint one “projected to continue through 2030”. These are forecasts by interested parties, not filed facts — worth knowing, not a basis for accepting a term.
